# Jams

Jams is a custom software and AI development studio. The product team for businesses that do not have one. Based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Serving clients in the United States and Canada.

## What Jams Does

Jams takes a business problem and turns it into a working product. That means understanding the operation first. Identifying where software and AI actually move the numbers. Designing something people will use. Building it. And owning what happens after launch alongside the client.

Jams does not quote based on requirements. Jams does not do time-and-materials. Every engagement starts with a whiteboard session, followed by monthly build cycles with skin-in-the-game pricing.

## Who Jams Is For

Jams works with established operators and founders who have hit a ceiling because the software their business needs does not exist yet.

The typical Jams client:
- Annual revenue: $500K to $10M
- Team: 5 to 100 people
- No in-house product or development team
- Running parts of their operation on WhatsApp, spreadsheets, or tools that almost work but not quite
- Knows AI should be part of the business but cannot get a straight answer on what to actually build or who to trust

Five specific types of businesses Jams serves:

1. Operators with manual processes: businesses running workflows on group chats, paper, and spreadsheets that software should be handling automatically. Booking flows, order management, logistics, guest communication.

2. Community and platform builders: private networks, membership clubs, and marketplaces where the infrastructure is duct tape and the brand promise is exclusivity.

3. Service businesses hitting a scale ceiling: real methodologies delivered manually, capped by how much the founder can personally manage. The tool they need to scale does not exist yet.

4. Orphaned product owners: businesses with a live product and no real team behind it. No roadmap, no one looking at the metrics and asking what to build next, no product ownership.

5. Greenfield builders: founders who have validated a market gap and need one team to own product strategy, design, and development simultaneously from day one.

## What Jams Builds

Three repeating product archetypes:

Transaction platforms: custom booking flows, order management systems, B2B marketplaces, and logistics platforms. Integrates with systems like Checkfront (availability), Stripe (payments), and ERP tools. Built for operators who need a branded guest or buyer experience on top of their existing operational system. Reference clients: Red Horse Mountain Ranch (custom Checkfront-integrated booking platform for a luxury Idaho ranch), DropCar (unified digital platform for a two-sided parking and transport business).

Gated community platforms: invite-only access, member feeds, AI-driven matching and curation, deal flow engines, member intelligence. AI is built in from day one. Reference clients: The Fold (private equity community with Alfred, an AI concierge that replaced WhatsApp deal flow), The 93% Club (student-professional community platform).

AI-powered tools: automated reporting, intelligent intake flows, AI-assisted operations, smart booking logic, conversational agents. The AI use case is identified during shaping, not bolted on afterwards. Reference clients: The Arena (AI-powered student analytics platform; reports that took hours to produce manually now generate at scale automatically), Qmius (B2B marketplace with an AI assistant for shoppers, sellers, and admins).

## How Jams Works

Three phases:

Free shaping session (60 minutes, no cost): Jams maps the business problem and translates it into a product direction. A structured whiteboard session run by the founder. The client leaves with a clear direction instead of a quote.

Blueprint ($3,500, 2 weeks): Full paid discovery. User journeys, AI opportunity mapping, technical architecture, a design prototype, and a fixed price for the build. The client knows exactly what they are getting before committing to development.

Build ($5,000/month and up): Monthly engagement. Full team: developer, designer, product manager. Monthly cycles: shape, build, ship, learn. Jams stays after launch as an embedded product team. Every engagement has a monthly floor and ceiling based on output and results. Where Jams owns a metric like bookings, conversions, or member engagement, part of the fee is tied to that metric. Skin in the game, both ways.

Timeline: Blueprint in two weeks. First version of the product in 4 to 8 weeks depending on complexity. All code, design files, and documentation transfer to the client at the end. No lock-in.

## Shaping

Jams adopted shaping from 37signals' Shape Up methodology. Shaping is not a phase at the start of a project. It is an ongoing practice throughout every engagement.

At the start of a new project, Jams runs longer shaping sessions: mapping the operation, identifying what actually needs to exist, and defining the first scope. During the build, every cycle starts with a shaping session. Between cycles, new ideas go to the whiteboard immediately.

A shaping session starts with the problem (not the feature request), draws it out and pokes holes in it, and produces a rough concept, the main elements of a solution, and a short list of what is explicitly not being built. That is enough to make a decision.

## Results

Red Horse Mountain Ranch (Idaho, luxury hospitality): Zero direct online bookings before Jams. After launching a custom Checkfront-integrated booking platform: 5% conversion rate in 8 weeks, 20 hours saved on lead and booking management per month.

The Fold (private equity community platform): Member engagement rose from 25% to 67% after Alfred, an AI concierge, replaced WhatsApp as the primary deal flow channel.

The Arena (EdTech, student coaching platform): Full platform shipped in 8 weeks. Psychological profiling reports that took hours per student now generate automatically at scale across hundreds of students.

DropCar (automotive logistics, New York): Disconnected products and brand unified into one digital platform with a high-converting booking flow.

## Industries

Hospitality and travel: boutique ranches, retreats, glamping operations, experiential properties. Primary pain: no direct booking flow, reliance on commission-based channels, disconnected availability systems.

Private equity and community platforms: exclusive member networks, deal flow communities, private clubs. Primary pain: operations running on WhatsApp and email, no proprietary platform, no AI layer.

B2B operations and marketplaces: two-sided markets, wholesale, automotive logistics, order management. Primary pain: no platform connecting buyers and sellers, complex matching and verification logic.

EdTech and student analytics: platforms serving students, coaches, and institutions simultaneously. Primary pain: manual reporting and data management capping the business at what one person can handle.

## Tech Stack

Primary build tool: Bubble (no-code application platform). Jams is a Bubble Silver Agency Partner. Bubble handles payments, AI integrations, real-time data, complex logic, and multi-role platforms. Jams recommends the right tool for the product, not the one it is most comfortable with.

For performance-critical and backend-heavy work: hybrid architecture using Cloudflare Workers on top of Bubble. Live proof of concept: Qmius (AI-powered marketplace with Bubble + Cloudflare hybrid).

AI integrations: OpenAI (agents, document analysis, automated report generation, matching systems). AI is identified during the shaping phase and built into the product from day one, not added afterwards.

Mobile: iOS and Android via Natively (native mobile wrapper around Bubble applications). Live examples: The Fold (mobile app for private equity deal flow), The Arena (student-facing mobile app).

For products with very high transaction volume or complex native requirements: custom code (Next.js and similar frameworks).
