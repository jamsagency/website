## 1. High-Fidelity

"Good enough" is the enemy. We reject the ugly MVP. If it looks like no-code, we failed. We build products indistinguishable from million-dollar custom engineering.

## 2. Extreme Ownership

We don't say "that wasn't my ticket." We own the outcome. Words aren't noise — they are commitments. If we say it, we deliver it.

## 3. Skin in the Game

We don't just do tickets for clients. We fight for their business success. We spend their budget as if it were our own. We act like partners, not vendors.

## 4. Brutal Trust

We are brutally honest because we respect each other enough to tell the truth, even when it's uncomfortable. That's what real partnership looks like.
